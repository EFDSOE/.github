## EFDSOE Foundation 🌍

**Shaping the future. Preserving values. Driving sustainable impact for society and digital sovereignty.**

EFDSOE is an independent foundation based in Graz, Austria, dedicated to open innovation, digital sovereignty, and forward-looking technological and social initiatives.

---

### 🧭 Our Core Pillars
- **Digital Sovereignty & Open Source:** Supporting transparent, independent, and resilient software infrastructures.
- **Sustainable Innovation:** Fostering long-term technological solutions that serve humanity and the environment.
- **Education & Accessibility:** Making knowledge, tools, and open standards accessible to communities worldwide.
